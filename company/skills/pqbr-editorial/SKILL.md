# Editorial PQBR
## Intellectual work routing (t465)
Route deep research, weekly themes, ideation, scripts and SEO through NotebookLM using canonical prompts. Agent LLMs coordinate and validate; they do not independently author this content. Use Gemini Web only as a documented fallback/supplement when NotebookLM is unavailable. Do not use NotebookLM for video generation; Flow is primary, Vids is fallback, and later candidates follow ADR-010.

Procedimentos da trilha. Detalhes do papel em `docs/AGENTES.md` §2 e nas instruções do agente. Sempre em conjunto com `pqbr-contrato`.
