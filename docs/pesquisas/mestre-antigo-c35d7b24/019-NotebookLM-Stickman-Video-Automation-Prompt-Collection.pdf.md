1. Stickman Niche Research Prompt 
Use in NotebookLM after adding your research/channel sources: 
Based on the sources I provided, give me 20 niche ideas for starting a Stickman 
animation YouTube channel. For each niche: - Give the main niche - Give relevant
sub-niches - Explain what type of Stickman videos could be created - Explain why
the niche has potential - Suggest the type of audience it could attract 
Focus on niches that work well with educational, entertaining, explanatory, 
motivational, psychology, history, or self-improvement Stickman animation 
content. 
2. Clickworthy Topic Ideas Prompt 
After selecting a niche, use: I want to build a Stickman animation YouTube 
channel around this niche: [NICHE] Based on the sources in this notebook, 
generate highly clickable and engaging video topic ideas for this niche. Give me
[20/30/50] topic ideas. Focus on: - Strong curiosity - Problems people want 
solved - Relatable situations - Emotional triggers - Educational value - Topics 
suitable for Stickman animation 
Avoid generic or repetitive ideas. 
Example: 
I want to build a Stickman animation YouTube channel around self-improvement, 
specifically discipline. 
Generate highly clickable video topic ideas about discipline, productivity, 
motivation, and personal growth.
